# LNG Shipping Optimization

## Overview

This project investigates mathematical optimization and learning-based approaches for large-scale LNG shipping and maritime logistics problems.

The main objective is to **recover globally optimal solutions for large-scale shipping optimization problems**, addressing the computational challenges arising from complex operational constraints, combinatorial decisions, and uncertainty.

The project combines exact optimization methods, heuristic algorithms, stochastic programming, and statistical learning to develop scalable solution approaches.

New methodologies will be progressively introduced, including learning-based optimization and reinforcement learning, with the aim of improving computational efficiency while preserving solution quality and economic performance.

## Research Objectives

The project focuses on the following objectives:

- Develop mathematical formulations for complex LNG shipping and scheduling problems.
- Investigate methods for recovering globally optimal solutions on large-scale instances.
- Improve the scalability of exact optimization algorithms and assess the effectiveness of heuristic approaches.
- Incorporate uncertainty into shipping and operational planning through stochastic optimization.
- Explore statistical learning methods to accelerate optimization and improve decision-making.
- Investigate reinforcement learning and other data-driven approaches for sequential decision problems.
- Evaluate the economic value, computational performance, and solution quality of the proposed methodologies.

A central research question is how to combine mathematical optimization and learning-based methods to obtain high-quality, economically valuable, and computationally tractable solutions for large-scale problems.

## Methods

- Mixed-Integer Linear Programming (MILP)
- Variable Neighborhood Search (VNS)
- Hybrid optimization heuristics
- Statistical Learning for Optimization
- Stochastic programming
- Scenario-based decision-making

## Problem Description

The LNG shipping problem involves coordinating vessels, cargoes, and
transportation schedules while respecting operational constraints.

The optimization framework aims to identify feasible and economically
efficient shipping plans.

Potential constraints include:

- Vessel availability and capacity
- Cargo delivery requirements
- Loading and delivery time windows
- Vessel routing and scheduling
- Operational and compatibility constraints

The exact formulation and assumptions will be documented as the
implementation develops.
